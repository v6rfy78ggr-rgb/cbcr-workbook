# Source attribution

Exchange-rate observations originate from the European Central Bank (ECB), downloaded on 6 November 2026 from [the historical reference-rate dataset](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist.zip). The included CSV is a selection of 2025 USD and CNY observations. Conversion rates and averages are derived calculations; their method is stated in `examples/methodology/FX_Method_and_Source.json`. They are not a separate ECB publication.

ECB data is available free of charge from its website. Reuse must retain accurate attribution and identify modifications. If incorporating it in sold products, inform buyers of the free original source. See the [ECB copyright terms](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html). These terms continue to govern ECB material; the repository MIT license covers original templates, examples and documentation. No ECB endorsement is implied.
